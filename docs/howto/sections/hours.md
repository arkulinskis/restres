Guests can only book when you are open, so get your hours right first.

### Regular hours and periods

A **period** sets the hours for each weekday between two dates, or for the whole year. You can have more than one: when periods overlap, **the shorter one wins**. So a "Summer" period overrides "Regular hours" for those dates. Press **Edit** on a period to change it, or **Add a period** for a new one. Two openings in one day (lunch and dinner) are fine.

The calendar on the right shows the hours that apply on each day, month by month.

![Opening hours and the calendar](shot:hours-page)

### Special days

Closed for a holiday, or open late for New Year's Eve? Press **Add a special day**, pick the date (and an end date for several days), give it a label, then choose **Closed** or **Special hours**. Special days override every period.
