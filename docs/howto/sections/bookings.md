### A booking by phone or in person

1. On **Reservations**, press **New booking**.
2. Pick the date, the time and the number of guests. **Stays for** is how long the table is held.
3. Leave **Table** on **Best free table**, or pick one. The line under it shows the best fit and how full you'll be.
4. Add the guest's name, phone and email. **Taken by** records how the booking came in.
5. **Guest's requests** is what the guest asked for. **Staff note** is for the team only: guests never see it.
6. Leave **Email the guest a confirmation** ticked to send one (when there's an email address), then save.

![Adding a booking](shot:book-editor)

If the time is full, PlanPlate says so and offers **Save anyway** for when you know you can squeeze them in.

### A walk-in

Press **Walk-in** and tap the number of people. They are seated now, at the best free table. To add a name or a note, press **Add a name or a note** instead.

![One tap seats a walk-in](shot:book-walkin)

### Change, cancel or mark a no-show

Tap any booking to open it. From there you can change the details or press **Approve**, **Decline**, **Seat now**, **Finished**, **No-show** or **Cancel booking**. **Call** and **Email** links reach the guest, and **Guest link** opens the page the guest sees. A booking cancelled by mistake can be brought back with **Restore booking**.
