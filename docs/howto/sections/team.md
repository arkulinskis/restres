Owners and managers can give someone access with just their email address.

1. Open the restaurant and go to **Settings**, then **Team** (the last item in the list on the left).
2. Type the person's email address.
3. Pick **Staff** for a waiter or host, or **Manager** for someone who should also change settings.
4. Press **Invite**.

![Inviting a waiter in Settings, Team](shot:team-invite)

PlanPlate emails them an invitation. When they sign in, or create an account, **with that same email**, the restaurant appears in their list. Until then they show as invited.

### Change or remove access

Each person on the team has a role menu and a remove button. Change **Staff** to **Manager** (or back) and it applies the next time their page loads. Remove someone who has left and they lose access right away. The owner's row can't be changed, and neither can your own.
