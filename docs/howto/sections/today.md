**Today** is the restaurant's front page: whether you are open right now, and what the day looks like.

- **Open now / Closed**, with today's hours. Managers can press **Change today's hours** or **Close today** for a one-off change, such as a burst pipe or a private party.
- **Four numbers**: bookings today, guests today, guests in the room now, and requests waiting for approval. Tap one to open Reservations.
- **Who's coming**: the next arrivals. Tap **Seat** when a party arrives and **Finish** when they leave.
- **Photo of the day**: press **Post** to share today's dish or the terrace in the sun. It shows at the top of your website for 3 days.
- **Specials**, **Coming up** (events) and **The week ahead** fill the rest of the page.
