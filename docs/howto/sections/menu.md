**Menu** holds your dishes in sections (Entrées, Plats, Desserts...) with prices and dietary labels such as V (vegetarian) and GF (gluten-free). The guest menu that the table QR code opens, your website and the menu widget all update the moment you save.

- **Add a section**, then add dishes to it. Use the pencil to edit a dish and the arrows to change the order.
- When a dish runs out, press **Sold out**. Press it again when it's back.
- **See the guest menu** shows the menu exactly as guests see it.
- Have a PDF or a photo of your menu? Use **Upload a PDF or photo** further down the page.

> Dishes of the day go in **Specials** on the Today page. They appear on your website when you add them and disappear when they end.
