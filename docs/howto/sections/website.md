PlanPlate can build a full website for your restaurant from what it already knows: your name, address, hours, menu, photos, specials and events. They stay up to date by themselves.

1. Open **Website** and press **Start from my details**. It takes about 10 minutes. To see a finished one first, press **See an example first**.
2. Work through the steps on the left: **Web address & colours**, then the texts and pictures. The preview on the right shows the site on a **Computer** or a **Phone**.
3. Press **Save**, then **Publish** to put it live. **Open** shows the live site, **Download** gives you a copy of the files, and **Unpublish** takes it down.
