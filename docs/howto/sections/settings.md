**Settings** is one long page. Use the list on the left to jump to a part, and press **Save changes** in the bar at the bottom when you are done (**Discard** undoes your changes).

![Settings](shot:settings-page)

- **Details**: the name, description, address and contact details guests see.
- **Booking rules**: **Table time** (how long a booking holds its table), **Time slots**, **Last booking** before closing, **Minimum notice**, the **Booking window** (how many days ahead) and the **Largest party online**. You can also pause online booking here; guests are then asked to call.
- **Approvals**: when a booking confirms itself and when it waits for you (below).
- **Booking form**: a note shown on the form, such as "We hold tables for 15 minutes", in each language.
- **Emails**: whether guests get emails, the address that receives approval alerts, and the wording of the confirmed, waiting and declined messages.
- **Languages**: which languages the booking form offers (English, Français, Latviešu).
- **Team**: who has access (see "Give someone access by email").

### Approvals

Bookings confirm straight away while there is room. Past these limits a manager decides:

- **Auto-confirm until**: once this share of seats is taken at a time, new bookings wait for approval. 100% confirms everything that fits, and 0% sends every booking to you.
- **Large parties**: parties bigger than this always wait.
- **When the limit is reached**: take requests for you to approve, or stop online booking for that time and invite guests to walk in.
- **Read requests first**: tick **Wait for me** to approve every booking where the guest wrote a request (an allergy, a high chair, a birthday).
