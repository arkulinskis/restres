Tap the circle with your initials (top right, or **You** on a phone) to open your profile.

- **Your name**: shown to the rest of the team.
- **Appearance**: follow your device, or always **Light** or **Dark** (dark is easier on the eyes in a dim dining room).
- **Password**: **Email me a reset link** to choose a new one.
- **Submit feedback**: tell us about a problem or an idea.
- **Sign out**: always sign out on a shared device.
