**Share & QR** has the QR codes and the plain links to send guests to PlanPlate.

- **QR codes** for booking a table, the menu and your website. Use **Download PNG** for table cards, the window, the door and flyers. The codes open your live pages, so you never need to reprint them when the menu or hours change. There is also a ready-to-print A4 sheet of four table cards.
- **Links to share** are for your social pages, your Google Business Profile, messages and email signatures.

The codes you paste into a website (the Reserve button, the menu, opening hours, the gallery) are on the **Elements** tab.
