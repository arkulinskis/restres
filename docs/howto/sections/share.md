**Share & QR** has everything you need to send guests to PlanPlate.

- **QR codes** for booking a table, the menu and your website. Use **Download PNG** for table cards, the window, the door and flyers. The codes open your live pages, so you never need to reprint them when the menu or hours change. There is also a ready-to-print A4 sheet of four table cards.
- **Share & embed** gives the links and codes to put the booking form on your own website, your social pages and your Google Business Profile.
