This part is for waiters and hosts. Your manager adds you by email (see "Give someone access by email"). You then sign in, or create an account, with that email.

The restaurant appears on your **Restaurants** page with a **Staff** badge. Press **Open** (Today) or **Bookings** (Reservations).

![A waiter's Restaurants page](shot:staff-dashboard)

You have three tabs:

- **Today**: who's coming next, with **Seat** and **Finish**.
- **Reservations**: approve or decline requests, take phone bookings and walk-ins, seat guests, finish tables, and mark no-shows.
- **Guests**: look up a guest's visits, tags and the team's notes before they arrive.

![Reservations as a waiter sees it](shot:staff-reservations)

### A typical service

1. Open **Reservations** on the host-stand tablet and leave it open. It updates by itself, keeps the screen awake, and chimes for new bookings.
2. When a party arrives, tap **Seat** on their row.
3. Walk-ins: **Walk-in**, then tap the number of people.
4. When a table leaves, tap **Finish**, so the table shows free again.
5. Someone didn't come? Open their booking and press **No-show**. The guest book remembers it.

Opening hours, tables, the menu and settings are for managers. Ask a manager if something there needs changing.
