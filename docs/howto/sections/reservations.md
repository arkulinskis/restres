**Reservations** is the page to keep open on the host-stand tablet. It checks for new bookings every minute and keeps the screen awake. With **Chime on** it plays a short sound when a booking or request arrives. Tap the page once first, because browsers only play sound after a tap.

### Requests waiting for approval

Bookings that need a decision appear at the top: for example, the restaurant was nearly full at that time, or the party is large. Each card says why it is waiting and how full you would be if you accept.

- **Approve** confirms the booking and emails the guest.
- **Decline** turns it down and emails the guest.
- **Details** opens the full booking, and **See the day** shows that date.

### Three views of the day

Use **Previous day**, **Next day** and **Today** to move between dates, then switch views:

- **Timeline**: one row per table across the day, with a red line for now. Click an empty spot on a table's row to book it at that time. Striped bookings are waiting for approval, and a star marks a regular.
- **List**: every booking of the day in time order. Each row has a one-tap next step: **Approve** a request, **Seat** a guest who has arrived, **Finish** a table that has left. Tap the row itself to open the booking.
- **Upcoming**: every booking still to come, from today onwards.
