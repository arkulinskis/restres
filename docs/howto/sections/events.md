Use **Events** for private parties, wine dinners or live music.

1. Press **Plan an event** and fill in the date, the times and a description.
2. Tick **Show it on our website** for a public event. For a private party, tick **Close online booking during the event**; staff can still add bookings by hand.
3. Press **Set up the room** to arrange tables just for that event: start from the everyday layout, move tables, and **Make one table** from several. **Save the room** when done, and **Print** gives you the plan for the team.
