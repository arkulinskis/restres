**Guests** is your guest book. Everyone who has booked is in it automatically, with their visits, how many people they brought, no-shows and cancellations. Search by name, phone, email or tag, and sort with the menu on the right.

![The guest book](shot:guests-list)

Tap a guest to open their card:

- **Tags** such as Regular, VIP, Allergy or Birthday. Tags show as badges on their bookings, so the whole team sees them.
- **Team notes**: "likes the corner table", "allergic to shellfish". The guest never sees these.
- **Visits and bookings**: every past and future booking.

![A guest's card, with tags and notes](shot:guests-card)

> Staff can look guests up. Only owners and managers can change tags and notes.
