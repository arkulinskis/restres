**Gallery** holds your photos and decides how they look on your website.

- Photos **in the gallery** show in the order you set: drag a photo by the grip in its corner, or use the arrows. The first ones are what guests see first. Use the eye button to show or hide a photo, and add a caption (it helps Google and screen readers).
- **Other photos and videos** are kept for specials, events and the website, but don't appear in the gallery.

![Your photos](shot:gallery-page)

### How it looks

Pick a **layout**: Mosaic (rows that show every photo whole), Masonry, Spotlight (one big photo and a strip), Slideshow (cinematic, with a slow zoom), Carousel (guests swipe), Editorial (a magazine spread), Grid or Polaroids. Each layout starts with the settings that suit it; then fine-tune the background, photo size, spacing, corners, frame, captions, hover and scroll effects, how many photos show before "See all", and whether a tap opens the photo full screen.

The **live preview** shows the real gallery, on a computer or a phone. Every change shows on your PlanPlate website and on every site with the gallery code at once. The code itself is on the **Elements** tab.
