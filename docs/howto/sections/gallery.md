**Gallery** holds your photos: your best dishes, the room, the terrace, the team.

- Photos **in the gallery** show on your website in the order you set. Use the eye button to show or hide a photo.
- **Other photos and videos** are kept for specials, events and the website, but don't appear in the gallery.
- **Gallery style** sets the look and the heading. Changes show on your website at once.
- **Put it on another website** gives you a code to paste into another site (in WordPress: a "Custom HTML" block). It always shows your latest photos.
