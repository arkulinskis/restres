**Elements** holds every piece of PlanPlate you can paste into your own website, grouped by what it does: **Booking**, **Menu**, **Opening hours**, **Photos** and **For developers**. In WordPress paste it into a "Custom HTML" block, in Wix into "Embed code", in Squarespace into a "Code" block. Each one always shows what is in PlanPlate right now, so you paste it once.

### The Reserve button

PlanPlate's own **Reserve a table** button, styled like a "Sign in with Google" button so it always looks professional. Pick a **Style** (Dark, Brass, Light or Outline), a **Shape** and a **Size**, and see it on a light or a dark site in the preview. Tap the preview: it opens your real booking form. Then **Copy** the code and paste it **once**, where the button should appear.

![The Reserve button and its styles](shot:elements-button)

- **Booking form inside a page** puts the whole form on a "Reservations" page, with nothing to click first.
- **Use your own button instead** (under "advanced") is only for a site that already has its own button. Paste it *instead of* the Reserve button, not as well.

### Menu, hours and photos

- **Live menu** shows your uploaded menu files (PDFs and photos) as buttons, then the dishes you typed in. Sold-out dishes hide themselves.
- **Live opening hours** follow your periods and special days.
- **Photo gallery** shows your gallery in the look you chose on the Gallery page. Change the look there and every site with the code follows.
