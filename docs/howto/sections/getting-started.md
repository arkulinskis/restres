PlanPlate runs in the web browser on a computer, tablet or phone. There is nothing to install.

### Sign in or create an account

1. Open PlanPlate and use the sign-in box on the home page.
2. New here? Choose **Create account**, enter your name, email and a password, and accept the terms.
3. Were you invited by your restaurant? Sign up with **the same email address the invitation went to**. The restaurant is already waiting in your list.

![Creating an account](shot:start-signup)

### Your restaurants

After signing in you land on **Restaurants**. Each card shows today's hours, bookings, guests and seats, plus a badge with your role (**Owner**, **Manager** or **Staff**). A yellow banner lists bookings waiting for approval, and the bell at the top shows the same count on every page.

- **Open** goes to the restaurant's Today page.
- **Bookings** goes straight to Reservations.
- **New restaurant** adds another restaurant to your account.
