It helps to know what your guests see, so you can answer their questions.

### The booking form

Guests reach it from your website, the **Book a table** QR code or the link on the Share page. They pick the number of guests, a day and a free time, then enter their name, email and phone. The form offers only times when you are open and have a table free. They can switch language at the top.

![The booking form: guests, day and free times](shot:guest-book)

After booking, the guest gets an email and a private link to their booking. There they can **change** or **cancel** it, and switch off PlanPlate news. If their booking needs your approval, they see that it is waiting, and they get an email when you approve or decline it.

### The guest menu

The **The menu** QR code opens your live menu, with prices, dietary labels and today's specials. Sold-out dishes disappear as soon as you mark them.
