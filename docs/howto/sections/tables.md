**Tables & layout** is a drawing of your room. PlanPlate uses it to give each booking the best free table.

- **Drag** tables to where they stand in the room.
- **Click** a table to change its name and seats, or untick **Available for bookings** to take it out of use.
- **Add a table** puts a new one on the plan.
- **Join tables** turns two or more tables into one bookable table for larger parties. Choose the hours and days the join applies, for example only Friday and Saturday evenings.

Use **Layout on** (the weekday) and **At** (the hour) to see the room as guests can book it at that moment.
