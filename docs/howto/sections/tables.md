**Tables & layout** is a drawing of your room. PlanPlate uses it to give each booking the best free table, and to push tables together for big parties.

- **Drag** tables to where they stand in the room. Tables that really stand next to each other should be next to each other on the plan.
- **Click** a table to change its name, seats, shape and area, or untick **Available for bookings** to take it out of use.
- **Joinable**: tick it for light tables staff can move and push together. Untick it for booths, bar seats and tables fixed to the floor; they show a small lock on the plan and are never moved.
- **Add a table** puts a new one on the plan.

### Big parties

When no single table fits a party, PlanPlate looks for **joinable tables side by side** (same area, close on the plan) that are free for the whole visit and pushes them together. If another party sits where the join is needed, it suggests **moving them** to another free table. Online, such a booking always **waits for your approval**: the request says exactly what to do ("Move Smith (2 at 19:00) from T4 to T7, then push T3 + T4 together"), and approving it does the move for you. Staff bookings made in the booking editor apply it straight away. If nothing fits, the time shows as full and you can take the party by phone.

**Try a big party** shows what PlanPlate would do for a party size, day and time, with the bookings already made, and lights up the tables on the plan.

![Trying a party of 8](shot:tables-bigparty)

**Always join some tables** is only for tables you keep pushed together at set hours (or a large table you split at quiet times). Choose the hours and days the join applies. Use **Layout on** (the weekday) and **At** (the hour) to see the room as guests can book it at that moment.
