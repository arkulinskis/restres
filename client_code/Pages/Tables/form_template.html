How PlanPlate seats a big party

  1. One table that fits (or tables you always join, below): booked straight away.
  2. Joinable tables side by side: free tables in the same area, close together on the plan, are pushed together. Online, the booking waits for your approval.
  3. Make room: if another party sits where the join is needed, PlanPlate suggests moving them to another free table. Approving the booking moves them for you.
  4. No room: the time shows as full. Take the party by phone and place them by hand.

Fixed tables (booths, bar) are never moved. Drag tables on the plan so neighbours really stand next to each other.
