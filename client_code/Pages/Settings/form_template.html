Details

Shown to guests on the booking form, the menu and their reservation page.

Time zone
All booking times are in this time zone. Search by country, city or GMT offset.

Coming from the old PlanPlate

If your website still has the old PlanPlate code (a booking pop-up from client.planplate.com, hours and menu boxes, the gallery), paste your old restaurant ID here. The old code then shows this restaurant's booking form, hours, menu files and photos, and new photos go into the same Cloudinary folder.

Booking rules

How guests can book online. Staff bookings in the portal can go outside these.

Approvals

Bookings are confirmed straight away while there is room. Past these limits a manager decides.

When the limit is reached
Take requests for you to approve, or stop online booking at that time and invite guests to walk in.

Booking form

Texts on the form your guests fill in.

Emails

Guests get an email when they book, when you approve or decline, and when a booking is cancelled. Each includes their private link.

Languages

The booking form, guest page, menu and emails come in these languages. Guests can switch between the ones you offer.

Also offer

Team

They sign in, or create an account, with that email and the restaurant appears in their list. We email them an invitation.

Delete restaurant

Removes the restaurant with its hours, tables, menu, guest book and every reservation. This can't be undone.
