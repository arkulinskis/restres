Why PlanPlate?

Built for independent restaurants.

Fast to set up, easy to run, and nothing you will never use. PlanPlate does reservations well and stays out of the way of service.

Ready in about 30 minutes

No developer needed. Enter your details and the booking button is ready for your site.

Run it from your phone

Every screen works on a phone or tablet, at the host stand or at home.

Staff trained in under 10 minutes

A handful of buttons covers a full service. Nothing to configure for them.

Help with the setup

We help you put the booking button on your website, free of charge.

Core features

Everything a busy dining room needs.

From one restaurant to many, the same simple tools for hours, tables, bookings, your guests and your website.

Every location, one login

Run all your restaurants from one account, each with its own hours, tables and team.

Roles for your team

Owners, managers and staff each see what they need. Staff handle bookings; managers set things up.

Walk-ins and phone bookings

Add a guest at the door or on the phone in a few taps, from a tablet or phone.

Bookings confirm themselves

Online bookings are accepted on the spot while there is room. You decide how full is full.

Room kept for walk-ins

Past 80% full, new bookings wait for your approval, or the time shows as walk-in only.

Alerts within a minute

When a booking needs a decision, your team sees it in the app and gets an email.

Hours that change with you

Seasonal hours, holidays and one-off closures, each taking over from the usual week.

Join and split tables

Push tables together for larger parties at the hours you choose, and split them again after.

Know your regulars

Every guest's visits, no-shows and notes in one place, so the team greets regulars by name.

Your website, built for you

Fill in one form and get a restaurant website with live hours, menu, gallery and the Reserve button.

Guest emails in your words

Confirmed, waiting and declined emails go out by themselves in English, French or Latvian.

Live menu and QR codes

Change hours or prices once and your website follows. The table QR codes never change.

For your guests

What your guests see.

Not a long form. A focused booking window on your own website that checks real availability and answers straight away.

  • Only shows times when you are open and have a table
  • Confirms on the spot, or tells the guest you will reply shortly
  • Sends a private link to change or cancel, and a calendar entry
  • Speaks English, French or Latvian
